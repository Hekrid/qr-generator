Privacy

Last updated 6 October 2026

This tool has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Most of it runs entirely in your browser. This page lists exactly what is stored, where, and how to delete it.

What never leaves your device

Regular QR codes (links, text, Wi-Fi, contacts, email, phone) are made in your browser. The text you type, the logos and background images you add, and the files you download are never uploaded anywhere.

Your browser's own storage on your device holds:

  • your recent QR codes (the history row), and your light or dark mode choice;
  • if you make dynamic links without an account, the keys that let you edit them;
  • if you sign in, a sign-in token so you stay signed in.

Clearing your browser data removes all of this. We can't see it.

What is stored on our server

Only two features use the server: dynamic links and the optional account. Their data is kept in a database hosted by Cloudflare.

WhatDetails
Each dynamic linkIts short code, the destination address you entered, and the date it was made. Plus either your username (if you were signed in) or a random edit key (if you weren't).
Each accountYour username, a salted hash of your password (the password itself is never stored), the list of your link codes, and the date the account was made.
Sign-in sessionsA random token for each device you're signed in on. Deleted when you log out, and expires after 60 days either way.
Failed sign-insA count of wrong passwords per username, used to block password guessing. Deleted automatically after 15 minutes, or as soon as you sign in successfully.

What is never collected

  • Email addresses
  • Names or phone numbers
  • IP addresses
  • Scan counts or who scanned
  • Location or device data
  • Analytics or tracking cookies

When someone scans a dynamic link, the server looks up the destination and sends them there. Nothing about the scan is recorded.

To slow down password guessing and spam, the server briefly counts requests per IP address (for example, at most 10 sign-in attempts per minute). Those counters live only in Cloudflare's memory for about a minute and are never written to our database.

Services this site relies on

Like any website, these services receive your IP address when your browser contacts them, and may keep it in their own logs under their own policies:

  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub's privacy statement
  • Cloudflare runs the server for dynamic links and accounts, and delivers one code library (cdnjs). Cloudflare's privacy policy
  • unpkg delivers the QR styling library.
  • Buy Me a Coffee, only if you click the donate button. Their privacy policy

Deleting your data

  • One link: press Delete next to it under "Your dynamic links". It's removed from the server right away and its QR code stops working.
  • Your whole account: sign in, then choose Delete account next to your username. This immediately deletes your account, all of its links and all of its sign-in sessions. There's no waiting period and no copy is kept.
  • Links made without an account: delete them from the same browser you made them in, since only that browser holds their edit keys.

Otherwise, data is kept until you delete it, so your dynamic QR codes keep working.

Your rights and contact

You can see everything stored about your account in the "Your dynamic links" list, change it there, and delete it at any time as described above. For any other privacy question or request, including rights under the GDPR, open an issue on GitHub.
